PLAN BATCH EXECUTION — a subagent dispatch is about to happen.

The unit of dispatch is a chain, not a task.

1. A dependency line T1 -> T2 -> T3 is ONE dispatch, not three.
2. k same-shape edits with no dependencies among them are ONE dispatch, whatever k is.
3. A leaf (docs, a changelog, a constant) never gets its own dispatch. Pack it into an
   existing slot.

One chain of 5 tasks or fewer: execute inline and dispatch nothing.
Wall clock floors at the critical path. Never split the critical path.
Gates (full suite + review) belong at chain hand-off points, plus one final. Not after
every task.

If the dispatch count equals the task count, stop and re-schedule.
Read the plan-batch-execution skill for the full rules and the required schedule format.
